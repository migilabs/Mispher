# Mispher

A personal recorder that turns conversations captured on Michele's Apple devices into a German Protocol with Action items.

## Language

### Capture

**Recording**:
Everything captured from Start to Stop. One Recording yields one Protocol.
_Avoid_: Aufnahme, session, conversation, capture

**Segment**:
A continuous stretch of a Recording from one Audio source. When the Audio source changes or an interruption pauses capture, a new Segment begins and the Recording continues.
_Avoid_: Stück, piece, chunk

**Audio source**:
The microphone a Segment was captured with: iPhone mic, AirPods mic or Watch mic.
_Avoid_: Quelle, input, source

**Marker**:
A line typed during a Recording, stamped with its time. The Protocol is built around Markers.
_Avoid_: Stichwort, note, bookmark

**Keyword**:
A name or term passed to transcription so it is recognised and spelled correctly.
_Avoid_: Stichwort, hint, vocabulary

### Output

**Transcript**:
The text of a Recording as returned by the transcription model.
_Avoid_: Transkript

**Protocol**:
The German document Mispher writes from a Recording. It always has a Summary, Decisions and Action items, plus any sections its Template adds.
_Avoid_: Protokoll, minutes, notes, summary

**Summary**:
The Protocol section stating what the Recording was about and where it ended up.
_Avoid_: Zusammenfassung

**Decision**:
Something the participants settled, as recorded in the Protocol.
_Avoid_: Entscheid, Entscheidung

**Action item**:
A follow-up recorded in the Protocol, with an owner and an optional due date.
_Avoid_: Aufgabe, task, todo

**Template**:
The instructions that shape the Protocol for one kind of Recording: which sections it adds to the core three, and their order, length and tone. Customer meeting, Internal meeting and Voice note are Templates.
_Avoid_: Vorlage, preset, type
